The Book

Renaissance of the Poor Soul is a spiritual memoir about suffering, waiting, and finding rest. It is not a book written from a pulpit. It is a book written from the wilderness – from the crossroads of desperation, frustration, and confusion. From Park Station. From hospital waiting rooms. From the long nights of unanswered prayer.

The book follows five movements: Displacement, Confrontation, Cracking Open, Reframing, and Rest. It draws on the Psalms, Lamentations, the words of Jesus, and the author's own hard-won experience to speak to every soul that has been afflicted, lost, restless, and still, somehow, refused to stop believing.

It is for the person who has confused a difficult season with a permanent sentence. It is for the person who needs someone to sit with them in the wilderness, not to fix it, but to say: I have been here too. And there is a way through.

The Author

Halalisani Ngema was born in Mandeni, on the North Coast of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa – a place where the soil is deep and the spirit runs deeper. He grew up in a home where faith was not spoken of formally, but was felt in the most sacred of places: in his grandfather's voice when he prayed, in the reverent tone of a man calling on the ancestors before a ceremony, in the quiet awe of a child who heard the name of God for the first time under an open sky and never forgot it.

He began his working life as a waiter, then a filing clerk, before rising through the ranks of the banking sector. Along the way he navigated workplace bullying, multiple resignations, business failures, the death of his younger brother, the loss of his mother, the loss of his father, an alcohol problem, the collapse of a long relationship, and a scam that left him stranded at Johannesburg Park Station with nothing but a phone and the refusal to give up.

He has raised three children through all of it. He has kept talking to God through all of it.

Renaissance of the Poor Soul is not a book written by someone who has arrived. It is a book written by someone who is on the road – and who believes, with everything he has, that the road leads somewhere.
