Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how renaissance.liviyo.co.za ("the Site", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve the user experience, and provide information to site owners. Cookies may be "first-party" (set by the Site itself) or "third-party" (set by another domain, such as an analytics or payment provider).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for several purposes, including:

  • Strictly necessary cookies – required for the Site to function, such as security, authentication, and basic page navigation. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional cookies – to remember choices you make (e.g., currency or display preferences) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
  • Analytics and performance cookies – to understand how visitors use the Site (e.g., which pages are visited, how long visitors stay) so we can improve it.
  • Third-party cookies – set by services we use, such as font providers, analytics services, or payment/social platforms.

3. Cookies We Use

The table below describes the main cookies and similar technologies used on the Site. We use this information to provide the Site and to measure and improve its performance.

Strictly Necessary

  • Site preference storage (e.g., cookie consent choice) – used to remember whether you have accepted or declined cookies.

Performance & Analytics

  • Standard server logs capture technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, and pages requested. This data helps us keep the Site secure and reliable.
  • Font providers (e.g., Google Fonts) may set cookies or use local storage to deliver the fonts that make the Site render correctly.

Third-Party Services

  • Payment processors (e.g., PayFast, Gumroad, Amazon, Lulu) set their own cookies when you are redirected to complete a purchase. These are governed by the respective provider's cookie and privacy policies.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have several options to control cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings, including blocking all cookies, blocking third-party cookies only, or deleting cookies when you close the browser. The "Help" function in your browser should explain how.
  • Cookie banner/consent: Where required, we provide a banner so you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Do Not Track: You can enable "Do Not Track" in your browser where it is supported.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how the Site functions and may reduce your overall experience. The Site will operate, but some features may not work properly.

5. Consent

Where required by applicable law (including in South Africa and the EU), we obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. By continuing to use the Site after being presented with our cookie banner, you are consenting to our use of cookies as described in this Policy. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or contacting us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The most current version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.
