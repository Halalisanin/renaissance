2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Fulfil book orders and deliver digital products

  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent — where you have given explicit consent (e.g. newsletter subscription)
  • Contractual necessity — to fulfil a purchase or service you have requested
  • Legitimate interest — to improve our services and website

This policy is compliant with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors for transaction fulfilment
  • Email service providers for newsletter delivery
  • Analytics providers for website improvement
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities when legally required

All third-party providers are contractually bound to protect your data.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. These include SSL encryption, secure hosting, and restricted access to personal data. While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we follow industry best practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Transaction records are retained for 5 years as required by South African tax law.

Under POPIA and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate data
  • Object to the processing of your data for direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request data portability (GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at halalisani@liviyo.co.za.
