## 2. Amazon KDP (Kindle + Paperback)

Amazon is the biggest bookstore in the world. You need both the Kindle version and the paperback listed there.

### Step 1: Create Your KDP Account
1. Go to **https://kdp.amazon.com**
2. Click **"Sign in"** — use your regular Amazon account (the one you buy things with)
3. If you do not have one, click **"Create your KDP account"** and follow the steps
4. You will need:
   - Your legal name: **Halalisani Ngema**
   - An address (use your South African address)
   - A phone number
   - Your banking details for royalty payments
   - A tax interview — select **"Individual"** and fill out the **W-8BEN** form (this is the form for non-US people — you do not pay US tax, you just declare you are not American)

### Step 2: Tax and Banking (Do This Before Publishing)
1. In KDP, go to **"Account"** then **"Tax Information"**
2. Fill out the W-8BEN form:
   - Country of citizenship: **South Africa**
   - Tax Identification Number: your South African ID number (13 digits)
   - Address: your full SA address
3. For **banking** — go to **"Account"** then **"Getting Paid"**
   - Add your South African bank account
   - Amazon accepts international transfers — give them your bank name, branch code, and account number
   - Amazon pays in USD and your bank converts to ZAR

### Step 3: Publish the Kindle Ebook

Click **"+ Create"** then **"Kindle eBook"**

## Troubleshooting

**"KDP rejected my cover"**
- Use the KDP Cover Calculator at https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/cover-calculator
- Enter your exact trim size and page count
- Download the template
- Place your front cover on the template
- Add back cover text and spine text
- Save as a single PDF

**"Gumroad will not let me withdraw to SA bank"**
- Set up a Payoneer account at https://www.payoneer.com
- Link Payoneer to Gumroad as your payout method
- Payoneer sends money to your SA bank

**"My EPUB has formatting issues"**
- Open the EPUB in Apple Books or Google Chrome to preview
- If it looks broken, use the PDF version instead
- Upload `PDF/book.pdf` to Gumroad as the main file

**"I did not get my ISBN from the printer"**
- Your ISBNs are already assigned: 978-1-0492-8328-9 (print) and 978-1-0492-8329-6 (ebook)
- Enter these manually in each store — you do not need a barcode image for digital stores

**"I am stuck on the W-8BEN form"**
- This is the tax form for non-US authors
- Select "Individual" (not corporation)
- Country: South Africa
- TIN: your 13-digit SA ID number
- You are declaring you are not a US taxpayer
- This is normal and required — every non-US author does this
