POPIA Policy

How we handle personal information in South Africa.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Responsible party

The responsible party is Halalisani Ngema, trading in connection with Liviyo Press and the Renaissance of the Poor Soul website. For privacy questions or requests, contact halalisani@liviyo.co.za.

Why we collect personal information

We collect and use personal information only for reasonable, specific purposes, including processing and fulfilling orders, responding to customer-support requests, sending newsletters where you have consented, maintaining website security, and meeting legal obligations.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact details that you choose to provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Order and support information needed to provide a product or answer a request.
  • Payment status and transaction references supplied by PayFast. We do not receive or store your card number, CVV, or online-banking credentials.
  • Limited technical information, such as browser and device details, where necessary for security and site operation.

Storage and protection

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, access controls, and retention limits to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Payment processing takes place through PayFast and is subject to its own policies. We retain information only as long as needed for the purpose collected or as required by law.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions and limits in POPIA, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and unsubscribe from marketing communications. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za. The regulator's contact details and complaint process are maintained on its official website.
